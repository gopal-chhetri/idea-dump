Turn your idea backlog into a ranked build plan.

For developers with more side-project ideas than evenings. Add your skills once, dump your ideas, and Idea Dump scores each one on how well it fits you, how much work it is, and how different it is from everything else on your list.

How the score works

How it works

Ten minutes of setup, then every new idea is scored the moment you add it.

  1. Describe your skills

    A short summary plus the languages, frameworks and tools you know, each weighted 1 to 5. This is what "fit" is measured against.

  2. Dump your ideas

    Title, description, key features, who it's for. Rough is fine. Each idea is scored as soon as you save it.

  3. Work from the top

    The backlog is sorted by score. Pin a favourite or set a manual rank to override it, and rescore everything after you learn something new.

How scoring works

Rule-based and deterministic: the same idea and profile always give the same score, and you can see why.

score = 0.5 × fit + 0.3 × (100 − effort) + 0.2 × novelty

50% Fit
Words from the idea's title, description, features and use case are matched against your skills, weighted by proficiency.
30% Effort, inverted
Estimated from feature count and description length. Smaller ideas score higher, so things you can finish rise.
20% Novelty
Text similarity against your other ideas. Five variations on the same to-do app won't all land on top.
